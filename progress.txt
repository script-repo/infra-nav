## Summary
The first column is Sites. Prism Central is the row, and its next column is the clusters. Each cluster then opens VMs, hosts, storage containers, networks, and categories. Clicking a VM opens the console between the folders and the detail pane. Right-click offers power actions. Hide folders collapses navigation. Hosts show hardware and performance. Storage containers are listed on the cluster.
